## About

I'm the founder of [DodoPrep](https://dodoprep.com) and a full-stack engineer. I build AI products end to end, from the model and the API to the app people open every day. Most of my work sits where product meets applied AI: tutors that adapt to a learner, agents that act on someone's behalf, and forecasting systems with honest evaluation.

**Right now**

- Running **DodoPrep**, an AI study companion for iOS, Android and the web
- Building **Kart**, a trust layer for AI agents that spend money
- Finishing **LatticeX**, my final-year research project on S&P 500 forecasting
- Happy to talk about AI products, edtech and agentic commerce: naveed.ahmed.swe@gmail.com
